The captures were made with NVIDIA Isaac Lab/Isaac Sim and a Unitree Go2 simulation asset. They depict simulated behavior, not physical robot hardware. Consult the upstream installation and license guidance before reproducing full training.

The demo uses original evidence displays, the two explicitly mapped normal/F1 capture files, and offline synthetic English speech. There is no music.
